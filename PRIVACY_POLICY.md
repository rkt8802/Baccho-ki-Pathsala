# Privacy Policy for "Baccho ki Pathsala"

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

**Baccho ki Pathsala (बच्चों की पाठशाला)** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of young children and families. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application operates.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
- **No Personal Data Collected:** Baccho ki Pathsala does NOT require account registration, login, or personal user profiles. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit personal information such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or location data.
- **Child Privacy Compliance (COPPA):** Our app is designed strictly for early childhood education (Kindergarten to Class 1) and complies with child online privacy regulations.

## 2. Media Playback & Embedded Content
- Educational videos are loaded inside a sandboxed player via official YouTube iFrame embed services.
- External links, popups, and external app launches (such as Chrome or separate video apps) are strictly intercepted and blocked within the app.

## 3. App Permissions
- **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Required solely to load educational content and video streaming within the embedded player.
