## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
Welcome to **Baccho ki Pathsala (बच्चों की पाठशाला)** — a child-safe early childhood education app designed for Kindergarten (Nursery, LKG, UKG) and Class 1 learners.

Powered by Ranjeet Thakur, this application brings together animated stories, phonics, rhymes, counting, social science, and general knowledge in an engaging, ad-free, and sandboxed learning environment.

---

### 🌟 Key Educational Modules

1. 🔤 **English & Phonics:**
   - A to Z Phonics & alphabet songs
   - Moral animated stories (The Tortoise and the Hare, The Thirsty Crow)
   - Sight words and sentence reading for Class 1

2. 🔢 **Math & Numbers:**
   - Counting 1 to 20 with catchy rhymes
   - Learning basic shapes (Circle, Square, Triangle, Rectangle)
   - Visual addition & subtraction with fruits and balloons
   - Tens and Ones place value concepts

3. 🌍 **Social Science & World Knowledge:**
   - My Family & Myself
   - Our Community Helpers (Doctors, Teachers, Farmers, Firefighters)
   - Good manners, hygiene, and clean habits

4. 💡 **General Knowledge (GK):**
   - Wild & farm animal sounds and 3D visual identification
   - Four seasons (Summer, Winter, Monsoon, Spring)
   - Human body parts & the 5 senses

---

### 🛡️ Child Safety & Safe Video Engine

- **Sandboxed Embedded Player:** Videos play seamlessly inside the application without opening external web browsers or separate video apps.
- **No Popup Ads or Redirects:** External link navigations are suppressed to protect young learners.
- **Bilingual Interface:** One-tap toggle between English and Hindi (हिंदी).
- **Parental Lock Gate:** Simple math verification protects settings and exit routes.

---

### 📱 Target Age Group
- Nursery, LKG, UKG (Ages 3 to 5)
- Class 1 (Ages 5 to 7)
